Back from Mexico on 26 Aug. Current stack is Beelink → Nano → BNO085. An IM10A IMU is due 25 Sep.

Lower body is standing. Perception hardware is still inbound: RPLIDAR C1, a 7" screen, and an MMlove synchronized global-shutter stereo camera — dual frames in one output, 3200×1200 at 60 fps or lower resolution at 120 fps, about 80 mm wide and 86 g. IFWATER is the other synchronized global-shutter option if that board slips.
