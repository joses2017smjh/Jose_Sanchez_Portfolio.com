title: Humanoid Locomotion & Navigation
summary: Evaluate learned humanoid locomotion across Isaac Lab and MuJoCo with matched controls and release gates.
problem: >-
  A high training reward can hide a walking policy that fails in another simulator.
  Locomotion needs repeatable checks across disturbances and terrain before a
  checkpoint can be trusted.
approach: >-
  Trained PPO policies in Isaac Lab, exported ONNX, and evaluated them with the
  upstream deployment controller in MuJoCo. Built randomization and sensor ablations,
  lidar mapping, and scripted A* navigation using oracle pose and goal. Qualification
  gates require both turning and straight walking to pass. These are sim-to-sim
  studies; learned locomotion has not transferred to hardware here.
result: >-
  October 6 confirmation: default randomization records 0/360 flat falls versus
  77/360 without it. October 8 navigation: 259/288 goals versus 247/288 baseline,
  with 0 versus 9 falls. Shared layouts are clustered observations; navigation
  uses oracle pose/goal. These are published simulation results, not hardware validation.
