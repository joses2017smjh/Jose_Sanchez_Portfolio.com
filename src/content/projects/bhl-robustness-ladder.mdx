title: Humanoid Robustness Ladder
summary: Train humanoid policies in Isaac Lab and check their transfer in MuJoCo, with failures kept in
  the record.
problem: Training reward alone cannot establish that a walking policy transfers to another simulator.
  The project needs repeatable evaluation across disturbances and terrain. High training reward can still
  hide transfer failure, which is why retractions stay in this record.
approach: Trained PPO policies in Isaac Lab, exported ONNX, and scored checkpoints with upstream's
  deployment controller in headless MuJoCo. Built randomization and sensor ablations, lidar mapping,
  scripted A* navigation, and task-specific qualification gates. Navigation uses oracle pose and goal.
  October gait-clock studies compare actor-visible and critic-only inputs across three training seeds;
  turning and straight walking must both qualify. Failed experiments and retractions stay in the ledger.
result: 'Biped transfer: 21/90 falls without randomization vs 0/90 with the default. Lidar mapping plus
  scripted A* reaches 24/24 unseen biped mazes; a qualified humanoid reaches 12/12 fresh layouts.
  October 2 actor-clock recipe fails: only 1/3 seeds also passes straight walking. Simulation only.'
