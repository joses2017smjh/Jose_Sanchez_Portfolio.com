title: Humanoid Robustness Ladder
summary: >-
  Train a 3D-printed humanoid in Isaac Lab. Score it in MuJoCo across 89 policies and 6,348 episodes.
problem: >-
  A policy that only works where it trained has learned PhysX, not locomotion. Upstream
  ships flat-ground control with no curriculum and no way to score a policy. High
  training reward can still hide transfer failure, which is why retractions stay in
  this record.
approach: >-
  Trained PPO policies in Isaac Lab (PhysX, 4,096 envs), exported ONNX, and scored them
  with upstream's own RlController in headless MuJoCo so observations match the
  sim-to-real path. Swept randomization, push and terrain curricula, ray-cast depth,
  then cloth-sort and cooperative lift. Gates refuse a verdict without a control. Four
  earlier claims in this same repository were retracted and left public.
result: >-
  Transfer inverts the training-reward ranking: unrandomized policies fall 23% of the
  time in MuJoCo versus 0% for the repo default. With arms, falls drop to 11.7% at d=1.0
  where the biped falls 37.8%. The best cooperative cube rollout lifts 7.8 cm, then the
  pair drops 41 cm before contact. Sim-to-sim only; hardware locomotion is not claimed.
