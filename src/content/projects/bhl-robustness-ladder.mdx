## Training reward and transfer can disagree

The flat-ground study covers five settings and three trained policies per setting.
Default randomization transfers better than the unrandomized configuration even
when training reward suggests a different ranking. The **0/360 versus 77/360**
comparison belongs to the 12-DoF biped study, not to the new full-body clip.
[Confirmation evidence](https://github.com/joses2017smjh/bhl-robustness-ladder/blob/main/campaigns/20261006-confirmatory/results/dr_verdict.json).

## Later R1HO qualifies across three fine-tuning seeds

The command-latched heading recipe passes the declared joint qualification gate
for **3/3** fine-tuning seeds: **30/30 turns**, **9/9 straight walks**, and push
falls of **8/60, 9/60, and 8/60**. These are 22-DoF simulated policies, with
simulator yaw and independently seeded fine-tunes of one selected R1H-s2 parent.
They use existing benchmark cases, not a new hardware or from-scratch study.
[H4 report and source records](https://github.com/joses2017smjh/bhl-robustness-ladder/blob/main/docs/H3_H4_CAMPAIGN_2026-10-08.md).

Earlier actor-clock R1 qualified only one of three seeds and failed its recipe
gate. That negative result remains in the
[October 2 record](https://github.com/joses2017smjh/bhl-robustness-ladder/blob/main/results/repo-gpu-20260923/turngait-r12-20261001/verdict/R1.json).

## Terrain control on held-out mild courses

A separate native FAST-LIO2 study recorded **180/180 clean goals** with heading
pursuit versus **19/180** for the baseline, within **480 total trials**. It uses
three frozen 12-DoF actors and 20 reserved reset seeds on flat ground, 1 cm
steps, and 3° ramps. Estimated pose feeds control; evaluator truth is separate.
This cohort is distinct from the 11° ramp and relief visualizations above.
[Terrain confirmation](https://github.com/joses2017smjh/bhl-robustness-ladder/blob/main/docs/TERRAIN_CONFIRMATION_2026-10-11.md).

## Corrected stereo pose and a retracted ice claim

Isaac Lab 3.0 read camera quaternions as `(x, y, z, w)` after they were written
`(w, x, y, z)`. Correcting the order pointed stereo down at the terrain. The
earlier ice-hazard result is retracted: patches spawned a median 72 m away.
The [findings ledger](https://github.com/joses2017smjh/bhl-robustness-ladder/blob/main/docs/FINDINGS.md)
preserves both corrections.

These are simulation results, not hardware validation: learned locomotion has not transferred to hardware.
