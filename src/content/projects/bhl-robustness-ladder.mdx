title: Humanoid Robustness Ladder
summary: Train humanoid policies in Isaac Lab and check their transfer in MuJoCo, with failures kept in
  the record.
problem: Training reward alone cannot establish that a walking policy transfers to another simulator.
  The project needs repeatable evaluation across disturbances and terrain. High training reward can still
  hide transfer failure, which is why retractions stay in this record.
approach: Trained PPO policies in Isaac Lab (PhysX, 4,096 envs), exported ONNX, and scored them with upstream's
  own RlController in headless MuJoCo so observations match the sim-to-real path. Swept randomization,
  push and terrain curricula, ray-cast depth, then cloth-sort and cooperative lift. Gates refuse a verdict
  without a control. Four earlier claims in this same repository were retracted and left public.
result: 'Transfer inverts the training-reward ranking: unrandomized policies fall 23% of the time in MuJoCo
  versus 0% for the repo default. With arms, falls drop to 11.7% at d=1.0 where the biped falls 37.8%.
  The best cooperative cube rollout lifts 7.8 cm, then the pair drops 41 cm before contact. Sim-to-sim
  only; hardware locomotion is not claimed.'
