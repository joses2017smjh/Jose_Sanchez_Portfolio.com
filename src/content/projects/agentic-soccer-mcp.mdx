title: "Agentic Soccer Prediction over MCP"
summary: "Calibrated forecasts served by a LangGraph agent over MCP, with a GRPO staking policy and human approval."
problem: >-
  Betting markets are a strong probability oracle, so the interesting problem
  isn't beating them — it's producing forecasts that are calibrated,
  internally consistent across correlated markets, and honest about their own
  uncertainty, then serving them through an agent where every number traces
  back to a tool call.
approach: >-
  Phase A is an offline stack: leakage-guarded features into XGBoost with
  isotonic calibration and split-conformal sets, a Dixon-Coles score grid
  reconciled exactly to its own marginals, shipped as one versioned artifact
  bundle. Phase B serves it — a FastAPI gateway into a LangGraph orchestrator
  driving MCP servers for odds, news and inference, with a human-in-the-loop
  interrupt before any staking suggestion. A GRPO-trained staking policy and a
  tunable-efficiency market simulator sit on top, tested against a fidelity
  ladder and six adversarial reward-hacking attacks.
result: >-
  On 102 World Cup 2026 matches, trained strictly pre-tournament: 0.9013 log
  loss and 62.7% accuracy against 47.1% for the prior, conformal coverage 0.951
  against a 0.90 target. The GRPO policy takes the best closing-line value of
  any policy that bets, +0.016. On EPL the closing line still wins.

      contradict another — sweep the parameters and every market updates in
      lockstep. A single prediction fires 11 tool calls across three MCP servers
      in 239 ms, and every number the agent shows traces back to one of them.

    body: >-
      A forecast that can't state its own reliability isn't much use. Running
      conformal coverage converges on its 90% target as matches stream in, and
      prediction sets widen to all three outcomes on genuinely uncertain
      fixtures and collapse to singletons on lopsided ones. The suggestion layer
      caps its own confidence to "low" whenever a pick falls outside the set.

    body: >-
      A 2,788-parameter MLP written from scratch in numpy maps per-fixture
      features to skip or back one outcome, trained with Group Relative Policy

      takes +0.016 mean closing-line value, 25 bp per bet above the favourite.
      A tunable-efficiency market simulator says where it transfers: eta = 0.85
      is the only rung that survives real data.

    body: >-

      seasons far better than policy reward: ECE drift +0.0004, but held-out
