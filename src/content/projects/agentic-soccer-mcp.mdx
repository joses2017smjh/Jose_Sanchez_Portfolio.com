summary: “Calibrated match forecasts served by a LangGraph agent across three MCP servers, with human approval before any stake.”
problem: >-
Betting markets provide robust probability estimates, making the primary challenge not outperforming them, but instead generating forecasts that are calibrated, internally consistent across correlated markets, and transparent regarding uncertainty.
The objective is not to surpass market accuracy, but to produce forecasts that are calibrated, internally consistent, and explicit about uncertainty.
Forecasts must maintain internal consistency across correlated markets and provide honest quantification of uncertainty.
Each forecast is served through an agentic system, ensuring every output is traceable to a tool invocation.
All numerical outputs are directly attributable to specific tool calls.
approach: >-
Phase A consists of an offline pipeline: features are guarded against data leakage and input to XGBoost, followed by isotonic calibration and split-conformal prediction sets.
A Dixon-Coles score grid is reconciled to its own marginal distributions, with additional layers for goal timing and player-specific propositions. The entire stack is versioned and bundled as a single artifact.
Goal-timing and player-prop layers are integrated to enhance granularity, with all components versioned for reproducibility.
Phase B exposes the artifact via a FastAPI gateway, orchestrated by LangGraph, which coordinates three MCP servers (odds, news/injuries, inference).
A human-in-the-loop interrupt is enforced before any staking recommendation, and conformal prediction sets are surfaced to the user.
The agent must present conformal sets, ensuring transparency in uncertainty quantification prior to any staking action.
any staking suggestion and conformal sets the agent must surface.
result: >-
Evaluation on 102 World Cup 2026 matches, using models trained exclusively on pre-tournament data, yielded a log loss of 0.9013 and accuracy of 62.7%, compared to 47.1% for the baseline. Conformal coverage was 0.951 against a 0.90 target.
On EPL data, the closing line outperformed the model, and this is explicitly reported.
Performance limitations are documented, and the model does not exceed market benchmarks.
