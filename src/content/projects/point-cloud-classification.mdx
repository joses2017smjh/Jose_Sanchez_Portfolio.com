  Point clouds are unordered. Classification needs to preserve useful local geometry while

  Implemented PointNet-style and attention-based classifiers and evaluated point-cloud

  desk/table confusion the dominant error. Graduate coursework: the figure comes from the
  training notebook's own saved output, and no deployment claim is made.
