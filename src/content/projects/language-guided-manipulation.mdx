## Learn the choice as well as the motion

The instruction selects the tumbler colour and destination table. SmolVLA
receives a rendered head-camera image, text, and proprioception, then predicts
chunks of base-velocity, arm, and gripper commands. A learned leg controller
executes walking while the arms and grippers track their commanded targets.
[Task and interface contract](https://github.com/joses2017smjh/bhl-robustness-ladder/blob/main/docs/WAITER_INTERFACES.md).

The first fine-tune scored **28/100** with each layout's instruction. Its
features held both the instruction's colour and which tumbler was which, but it
picked between the two at about chance. The choice is made in an episode's
first ten frames, **1.6%** of the training data. On development layouts,
**3.4×** the demonstrations alone left the choice at chance (**95 of 189**
pick-ups). With the same demonstrations, drawing **30%** of each training batch
from the first 12 frames made it right in **192 of 192**. Phase 3b, trained
that way, scored **75/100** on fresh layouts and **76/100** with the held-out
template.
[Program and results](https://github.com/joses2017smjh/bhl-robustness-ladder/blob/main/docs/WAITER_PROGRAM.md).

## Keep the stronger baseline

DSRL noise steering on the retrained policy reached **80/100** versus **74/100**
for its normal noise, failing the improvement gate (**p = 0.20**). Constant zero
noise reached **82/100**. The learned noise policy therefore did not justify
replacing the baseline.
[Phase 4b verdict](https://github.com/joses2017smjh/bhl-robustness-ladder/blob/main/results/waiter-20261005/phase4b-gate/verdict.json).

## What the demonstration establishes

This project tests a small, simulated instruction domain: two colours, two
tables, and six phrasing templates. The scripted expert reads oracle object positions
from the simulator. The learned policy uses rendered vision, text, and joint
state. The demonstration clip is a development-layout run, separate from the
scored evaluation.

A declared stance lock holds the base while the robot stands and releases it
for walking. The scripted expert scored **86/100** with this assistance and
**1/100** with a free base. Removing standing assistance remains unfinished;
these results do not demonstrate unassisted whole-body manipulation or
physical deployment.
