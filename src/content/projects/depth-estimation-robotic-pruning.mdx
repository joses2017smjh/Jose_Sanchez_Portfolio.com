title: "SPUR: Metric Depth for Robotic Pruning"
summary: From synthetic orchard data to metric depth models and a containerized inference service.
problem: >-
  A pruning robot needs distance estimates on thin branches. Outdoor sensor noise and
  monocular scale ambiguity make this difficult; real orchard ground truth is also expensive
  to collect.
approach: >-
  Generated Blender RGB, depth, masks, and calibrated camera poses. Compared monocular
  baselines, fine-tuning, and multi-view refinement under a fixed validation protocol, then
  packaged learned inference in FastAPI and Docker.
result: >-
  DINOv2 refinement reached 0.0445 ± 0.0057 m RMSE on synthetic validation (10 trees, five
  seeds). The PyTorch refiner measured 156 ms p50 on a V100 with FP16 and precomputed depth;
  this excludes the HTTP and base-depth stages.
