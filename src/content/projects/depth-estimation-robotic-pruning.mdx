## What sim-to-real actually costs

This project’s task-specific training and evaluation are synthetic. A
hold-out affine fit on the two paper validation trees puts DA2-ft at 0.0344 m
against Blender ground truth, and a TinyUNet trunk segmenter reaches 0.9295 IoU.
But running the same depth through *predicted* masks instead of ground-truth
ones moves error from 0.031 m to 0.112 m. That gap, not the headline, is the
number to beat.

Stereo fusion is not shipped: naive flow with guessed variance made things
worse, and epipolar gating left too few valid trunk pixels to score. A partial TensorRT fuse/decode plan is recorded at FP32 on RTX 8000.
There is no complete TensorRT encoder/DA2 pipeline or end-to-end deployment result.

The 0.0445 ± 0.0057 m refiner summary averages five saved best-validation
scores, not a fresh held-out test rescore.
