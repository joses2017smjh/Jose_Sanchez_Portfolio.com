title: "Vision-Based Metric Depth Estimation for Robotic Pruning"
summary: "Metric depth estimation for dormant orchard scenes, built to guide robotic pruning."
problem: >-
  Robotic pruning needs centimeter-accurate depth on thin, cluttered branches,
  where commodity sensors and off-the-shelf monocular models fall short.
  Collecting real orchard data with ground-truth depth at that precision is
  slow, seasonal, and expensive.
approach: >-
  Built a synthetic-orchard pipeline in Blender that renders RGB, ground-truth
  depth maps, masks, and full camera intrinsics and extrinsics for dormant
  apple tree scenes. Compared monocular, calibrated, fine-tuned, and
  multi-view RGB-D depth models on those scenes, then refined predictions
  with a DINOv2-based RGB+D multi-view model. Profiled deployment latency
  alongside accuracy to make the speed/precision trade-off explicit.
result: >-
  Validation RMSE improved from 0.0550 m to 0.0445 ± 0.0057 m with DINOv2
  RGB+D multi-view refinement. Latency: 484.7 ms per 6-view group (80.8 ms
  amortized per depth map) vs 333.6 ms per monocular frame — higher accuracy
  at the cost of a slower minimum inference unit.
