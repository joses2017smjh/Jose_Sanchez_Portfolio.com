title: Bimanual Garment Folding in Isaac Sim
summary: >-
  Port a camera-driven VLA into Isaac Sim and gate fine-tunes with paired settled-fold evaluations.
problem: >-
  A folding policy is easy to fool: replaying a demonstration looks like success, and a
  renderer that crashes looks like a modelling failure. The task needs a scorer nobody
  tuned, held-out garment poses, and a clear line between the policy acting and a
  demonstration being played back.
approach: >-
  Ported SmolVLA to PhysX cloth with three rasterised camera views and direct joint targets.
  Built recovery supervision, renderer-domain diagnostics, and source-pinned paired evaluations.
  Candidate and baseline run interleaved; the challenge checker scores folds after 600 actions
  and a 60-step settle. H10 replans after 10 actions; H50 executes the full 50-action chunk.
  Storm observations avoid the pinned RTX renderer crash. Demonstration replay stays separate
  from policy execution.
contribution: >-
  I built the simulator port, observation path, failure diagnostics, recovery-training
  adaptations, and paired evaluation drivers. Robot assets, garment assets, the challenge
  checker, and the SmolVLA backbone are upstream components.
result: >-
  September 27 v8: H10 settled folds improve to 9/16 from 2/16; H50 falls to 6/16 from 8/16.
  All 64 episodes complete, but the preregistered non-regression gate fails: baseline retained.
  Eight reused development poses; shared-seed repeats and GPU effects limit generalization.

    body: >-
      A demonstration replay folds the garment without the policy doing anything, so it
      proves only that the scene is solvable. Every number here comes from closed-loop
