title: "Bimanual Garment Folding: Research in Progress"
summary: "A folding-policy port and camera-health investigation. Strict evaluation has not shown an adaptation gain."
problem: >-
  A folding rollout can look plausible while its policy receives stale camera frames.
  The experiment needs fresh observations, held-out garments, and an external fold
  checker. A demonstration replay or a saved success label alone is not enough evidence.
approach: >-
  Port the policy and observation path into Isaac Sim, then grade closed-loop rollouts
  with the challenge's garment-fold checker. The latest evaluation checks camera
  freshness and treats rendering failures as experiment failures. Compare the baseline
  and adapted checkpoints on matching short-pants poses; retain incomplete runs instead
  of converting them into successful results. This is a research implementation of an
  external method, not a verified reproduction of the original challenge performance.
contribution: >-
  Built the Isaac Sim integration, observation-path diagnostics, evaluation adapters,
  and Slurm experiment workflow. Investigated stale-image failures and preserved both
  task failures and infrastructure failures in the results. The original policy method
  and challenge checker belong to their authors.
result: >-
  Strict short-pants evaluation: baseline 8/24, adapted seed 1 3/24; no improvement.
  Both score 0/4 on unseen garments. Only 5/12 full class/checkpoint evaluation cells
  completed; the remainder failed or timed out. A separate new seed-0 top rollout failed after 600 actions with 601 validated renders.
